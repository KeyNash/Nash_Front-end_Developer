# Portfolio content evidence

Public case-study copy follows this order of authority:

1. Current repository documentation, implementation, and test evidence.
2. Behavior observable in the current verified build.
3. Current live deployment.
4. Business facts explicitly confirmed by Nash.

## Publication safeguards

- Mahabu Media Services and Koromosho Springs are marked `live-stale`; their older deployments are not linked.
- Nexa Mobile and Tamu Kenya are marked `live-current` because their frontend demonstrations were verified on Vercel on 26 September 2026. Their case studies state that live backend, payment, delivery and fulfilment services are not connected.
- Aura Kenya is a fictional, local-session product walkthrough, not a live dating service.
- JuaDuka POS remains `in-progress`. Its private repository is not linked publicly; live Daraja/SMS are excluded, and the physical shop-day walkthrough, true 1–2 GB Android device gate, and full eight-hour soak remain open.
- Business metrics, user counts, revenue, testimonials, awards, conversion claims, and operational outcomes are absent unless a future approved evidence record supports them.

Detailed source references are stored in each object in `src/content/projects.ts` and shown at the bottom of each public case study.
